Where Falcon is today

Thirty years as Sunderland's largest independent computer retailer, 3,000+ products from £1 cables to £6,000 custom gaming PCs, a bespoke build service, and a retail move under way. The website and the shop currently run on two unconnected systems.

  • Websitefalconcomputers.co.uk runs on a bespoke platform with its own catalogue, basket, checkout and customer login.
  • ShopNCompass Advanced Retail from Finde Solutions: a Windows EPOS built for electrical retailers, with its own stock, sales and customer records.
  • The moveA new store means new tills, new hardware and a natural point to decide what the next ten years run on.
Today

Stock, prices and customers live in two places: the website and NCompass. Either they're maintained twice or a connector keeps them in step, and someone owns that connector.

With Shopify

One catalogue. Receive a delivery of graphics cards once and the website, the till and the reports all update. Sell the last one over the counter and it comes off the website within seconds.

Today

The EPOS is tied to the PC it runs on and a separate support contract. Moving shop means moving, reconfiguring and re-licensing the till setup.

With Shopify POS

POS runs on an iPad, iPhone or Android with a Shopify card reader. Shopify maintains the app, the hardware and the payment integration under the same subscription as the website. Moving shop means picking up the iPads.

Today

A customer who buys a monitor online in March and a PC in store in June is two separate people in two separate systems. Shop customers rarely make it onto the email list.

With Shopify

One customer profile across web and store. Staff send email receipts in a tap, and Shop Pay recognises returning customers from the card they pay with. In-store buyers become repeat online buyers for upgrades and accessories.

Today

Click and collect, deposits on custom builds, shipping a part from the shop and returns of online orders in store are handled around the edges of separate systems.

With Shopify POS

Buy online, pick up in store. Buy in store, ship to the customer. Draft orders with deposits for custom PC builds. Returns and exchanges at the counter regardless of where the order was placed. One gift card that works online and in store. All native, no workarounds.

Today

Trade and business customers see ex VAT prices on the site, but ordering on account at trade pricing is a phone call or a visit.

With Advanced

Shopify B2B is included on Advanced (up to 3 catalogues). Trade customers log in to the same site and see their own prices and payment terms. Same stock, same admin, no second website.

Today

Shop staff can't see what's happening online. Performance lives in NCompass end-of-day reports and whatever the website platform offers, separately.

With Shopify

Shop staff see online orders, abandoned checkouts and customer history at the till. You get sales by channel, product, staff member and hour in one admin, with Advanced's custom report builder on top. 15 staff accounts on Advanced, unlimited POS staff logins with roles and PINs on POS Pro.

Things to work through together

Shopify covers the shop and the website natively. These are the Falcon-specific pieces to scope properly before you commit, so there are no surprises.

Custom PC builder

Your bespoke system builder is a real differentiator. On Shopify it's rebuilt with a configurator or bundle app, or as custom theme work. We'll scope the right route with our Solutions Engineer and show you examples before you decide.

Repairs and service tracking

NCompass tracks items on repair. Shopify doesn't do this natively. Options are a repair-tracking app from the App Store or a lightweight workflow using draft orders and tags. We'll recommend one based on your repair volume.

Supplier stock feeds

If distributor price and stock feeds update your catalogue today, the equivalent on Shopify is an inventory sync app or a direct integration. Worth confirming which feeds matter before migration.

Part exchange and refurbished stock

Part exchange is handled at the till as a discount or as store credit on a gift card, and refurbished units are normal products with their own SKUs. Serial number tracking, if you need it, comes from an app.

Trade accounts

Shopify B2B on Advanced supports company accounts, catalogue pricing and payment terms. We'll map your current trade customers and terms into it so nothing is lost on day one.

Data migration

3,000+ products, customers and (optionally) order history move across by import. The POS offer's migration support covers this, and we'll agree what comes across from NCompass versus the website.

Suggested sequence

Timed around the retail move, so the offer's windows work for you rather than against you.

  1. Now: a working session to walk through the migration plan, confirm requirements (builder, repairs, feeds, trade) and agree the plan.
  2. Website build: Advanced store set up, products and customers imported, theme and the custom builder route in place. The current site keeps trading until you're ready to switch.
  3. When the new store is ready to trade: add POS Pro yearly. The 7-day hardware window and the 90-day setup and rebate windows start here, so don't switch it on early.
  4. Within 7 days: redeem the hardware code for the Victa Mobile reader.
  5. Within 90 days: setup call, POS configuration, staff training, go live, and trade through Shopify Payments. $10,000 USD of in-person sales in that window triggers the £155 rebate.
  6. Later: add a second location or a warehouse location at the same per-location price when needed.

Who qualifies: retailers new to POS Pro. Falcon qualifies as a new Shopify store that hasn't used POS Pro before. No revenue minimum.

Term: 12 months, billed monthly. Early cancellation means paying the remaining months. Auto-renews at the same discount with a reminder beforehand. Upgrading to Plus releases you from the remaining term.
